\chapter*{\Large \center Abstract}

The problem of automated fact verification has been researched heavily but is under-explored for semi-structured evidence such as tables. The relevant literature explains ways of retrieving evidence, representing tables and claims, and utilising language models to aggregate evidence to reach a fact verification conclusion. 

\vspace{\baselineskip}
\noindent The dissertation intends to explore novel ways of combining what is out there as well as original ideas from myself to utilise a task-specific language model to produce a more effective automatic fact verification pipeline.

\vspace{\baselineskip}
\noindent To date, I have gathered as baselines the simplest automated methods for fact verification. I first did logistic regression and then simple table and claim encodings and classification using several language models on the TabFact dataset with a claim and the entire table as evidence. Most have not done much better than random guessing, demonstrating how much more complexity is required to produce an effective automated fact verification method.
